Grim Festival

Deck size is 60 cards minimum.

4 of each card max except for basic lands.

Players start with 20 life.

Exactly 15 card sideboard required.

Play as best 2 of 3 in a 1v1.

Roll to see who goes first. Loser of game 1 decides who goes first for game 2. Loser of game 2 decides who goes first for game 3.

We will play round robin, for the tournament.

All cards must be from the "In Boosters" section of the scryfall link.

We will be using the London mulligan rule. Once you decide to keep your hand, you will place X cards on the bottom of your library, where X is the number of times you've taken a mulligan.
